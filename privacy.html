Privacy Policy

getdealtin.com · Privacy

What we keep. What we don't. Why.

getdealtin is built for families who are already being squeezed by systems that aren't straight with them. So we'll be straight with you. This page explains, in plain language, exactly what the tool keeps, what it never keeps, and why.

Effective date: October 26, 2026

The short version: We never ask for or keep your name, email, phone number, or home address — nothing that says who you are. We don't sell anything about you, ever, and we don't run ads. What we do keep is anonymous: the answers you give the tool, with no way to tie them back to you as a person.

What we keep — and why

When you use Make It Stretch, the tool saves the answers you type and the things you tap, so we can see how it's being used and spot patterns that help whole communities — for example, "a lot of families in this area are stretching very small grocery budgets." Here's what gets saved:

  • The numbers you enter — your grocery budget, how many people you're feeding, and how many days it needs to last. This is what lets the tool build your plan.
  • Your zip code — only to look up local food prices for your area and the right assistance programs. It is never used to find your exact address.
  • Your food choices — the cuisine you pick, where you shop, foods you avoid (like gluten or dairy), and whether you plan to use leftovers. This is how the tool fits the plan to your kitchen.
  • What you do in the tool — which meals you swap, items you check off, prices you correct, and links you open. This tells us which parts actually help.

None of this is attached to your name, your email, your phone, your address, or your device. There is no way for us — or anyone else — to look at a saved answer and know it was you.

The "visit code" — and why it isn't tracking you

Each time the page loads, the tool makes up a random string of letters and numbers for that one visit — think of it like a numbered coat-check ticket. It simply lets us group the steps of a single visit together ("someone set a budget, then swapped a meal, then printed a list") so we can see where the tool is confusing. The code is random, it isn't your name or your device, it isn't saved after you leave, and the next time you come back you get a brand-new one. It can't follow you around.

What we never collect

  • Your name, email, phone number, or any contact information
  • Your device ID, browser fingerprint, or IP address
  • Your home address or exact location — only the zip code you choose to type
  • Anything that could identify you as a specific person
  • Advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics like Google Analytics

How we share it — big picture only

We only ever share this data as big-picture totals — for example, to help a city or a food bank understand where help is needed most. Before any number is shared, we remove anything small enough to point at a single household, so no report can be traced back to one person. We do not sell your data, and we do not hand individual information to advertisers, data brokers, or government agencies. Full stop.

How long we keep it

Anonymous usage data is kept for up to 24 months so we can study trends over time, and then it's deleted. Because we never collect anything personal, there's no personal information to hold on to in the first place.

Children

getdealtin is not meant for children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.
