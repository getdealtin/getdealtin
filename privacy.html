Privacy Policy

getdealtin.com · Privacy

What we collect. What we don't. Why.

getdealtin is built for families already being taken advantage of by systems that aren't transparent. This policy says exactly what we collect, what we don't, and why.

Effective date: June 17, 2026

The short version: We don't collect your name, email, location, or any information that could identify you. No cookies. No tracking pixels. No Google Analytics. No data selling. Ever.

What We Collect

When you use Make It Stretch, we log anonymous usage events to understand how the tool is being used and to surface community-level insights. These events include:

  • Weekly budget amount and household size entered
  • Zip code you voluntarily provide
  • Food items searched or suggested by the tool
  • Brand comparisons clicked
  • Whether you select online retail vs. local grocer options

These events contain no name, email, device ID, or IP address. They cannot be traced back to you.

What We Do Not Collect

  • Your name, email, or any contact information
  • Your device ID, browser fingerprint, or IP address
  • Your location beyond the zip code you choose to enter
  • Any information that could identify you as an individual
  • Cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics

Cookies & Third-Party Tracking

No advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or platforms like Google Analytics that share data externally. We do not sell data. We do not share individual-level data with anyone — including advertisers, data brokers, or government agencies.

Data Retention

Anonymous event data is retained up to 24 months for trend analysis, then deleted. No personal data is retained because no personal data is collected.

Children

getdealtin does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy: getdealtin.hq@gmail.com — response within 5 business days.
